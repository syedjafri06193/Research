# Dr. Charity Flener Lovitt, Ph.D.

**Title:** Associate Teaching Professor of Physical Sciences
**Institution:** University of Washington Bothell
**Department:** Physical Sciences, School of Science, Technology, Engineering & Mathematics
**Email:** lovittc@uw.edu
**Phone:** 425.352.5055

---

Dr. Lovitt is an associate teaching professor at UW Bothell whose scholarship centers on transforming STEM education through evidence-based, student-centered pedagogy. Her research interests include belonging, curriculum design and assessment, information literacy, and green chemistry. She teaches courses in general and preparatory chemistry, as well as interdisciplinary science and Women in STEM. A two-time Fulbright awardee — having taught in Germany and Qatar — she has published extensively in chemical education journals and contributed to climate-focused and equity-oriented pedagogy.
