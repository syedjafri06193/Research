# Benjamin N. Grossmann

**Title:** Associate Faculty, Physics | Lecturer, Physics
**Institutions:** Cascadia College (Bothell, WA) | University of Washington Bothell
**Department:** Physics
**Research Affiliation:** Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

---

Benjamin N. Grossmann is a physicist and educator based in Bothell, Washington, with over a decade of experience teaching physics at the college level. He currently holds associate faculty appointments in Physics at Cascadia College and serves as a lecturer at the University of Washington Bothell, where he has also served as a faculty advisor to the Sigma Pi Sigma physics honor society. His research specializes in linear algebra and its applications to quantum information theory, with work including studies on maximally entangled states and matrix rank problems. He is affiliated with the Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL) and has contributed to data-driven control and system identification research. Beyond the classroom, Grossmann has served as a guest instructor at science education camps for youth and has been active in the Society of Physics Students (SPS) community.
