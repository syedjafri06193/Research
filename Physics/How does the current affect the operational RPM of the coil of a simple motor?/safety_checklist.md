# Pre-Experiment Safety Checklist

Complete all items before powering on any equipment.

---

## ✅ Equipment Check

- [ ] Power supply output is set to **0 V / 0 A** before connecting
- [ ] All wire insulation is intact — no exposed conductors visible
- [ ] Ammeter is set to the correct range (DC, appropriate amperage)
- [ ] Motor rated voltage/current is confirmed and **will not be exceeded**
- [ ] Tachometer battery is charged / fresh
- [ ] Reflective tape is securely attached and will not fly off at speed

## ✅ Setup Check

- [ ] Motor is secured firmly — cannot tip or move during operation
- [ ] All connections are tight (no loose banana plugs or crocodile clips)
- [ ] Ammeter is wired **in series** (not in parallel — this would short the supply)
- [ ] No flammable materials near the motor or power supply
- [ ] A clear, unobstructed path exists for the rotating shaft

## ✅ Electrical Safety

- [ ] Maximum current setting does not exceed motor rating
- [ ] Power supply has current limiting capability (not just voltage limiting)
- [ ] If the motor stalls or smokes, power supply will be switched off immediately
- [ ] Lab partner knows the location of the emergency power cutoff
- [ ] Hands are kept away from rotating motor shaft during operation

## ✅ During Experiment

- [ ] Allow motor to cool for ≥ 60 seconds between trials above 1.5 A
- [ ] If motor casing becomes too hot to touch (>50°C), **stop immediately**
- [ ] Do not exceed the motor's rated RPM (check datasheet)
- [ ] Keep tachometer sensor clear of the rotating shaft

## ✅ Emergency Procedures

- **Motor sparks/smokes:** Cut power immediately. Do not touch motor. Wait for it to cool.
- **Wire overheats:** Cut power. Identify the short circuit before restarting.
- **Supply trips:** Do not reset until the cause is identified.
- **Eye hazard (tape flies off):** Stop immediately. Wear safety glasses for subsequent trials.
